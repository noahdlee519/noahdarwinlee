Don’t rely on the atlas as an official, legal, or authoritative statement of any border, claim, or sovereignty, past or present, and don’t take a border’s presence or absence as a position on any dispute.

What the historian is. The historian is Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Everything it writes is fiction: invented history about an invented world. It can be wrong about the real history it starts from, it can contradict itself, and it can change your map in ways you did not expect; Undo reverses anything it changes. Don’t rely on it as a source for anything real.

CHRONIA

The world's borders from 3000 BC to today

An atlas of the world's states and peoples from 3000 BC to today. Move the year at the foot of the page, and click a state or people to read about it.

The borders come from dated maps, so between two of them the atlas shows the earlier one and says so. Before the mid-1600s most borders are approximate and are drawn as dotted lines. Peoples and cultures, as distinct from states, are left uncoloured and named in italics.

To redraw history instead, try UCHRONIA.

Keys: ← → the previous or next map | P plays the maps in order | M flat map | + and − zoom | Esc closes

Sources: historical-basemaps (A. Ourednik), Natural Earth, Terrain Tiles, and Wikipedia. All credits and licences

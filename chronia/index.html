CHRONIA

The world's borders from 3000 BC to today

An atlas of the world's states and peoples from 3000 BC to today. Move the year at the foot of the page, and click a state or people to read about it.

Until 1880 the borders come from dated maps, so between two of them the atlas shows the earlier one and says so. Before the mid-1600s most borders are approximate and are drawn as dotted lines, and peoples and cultures, as distinct from states, are left uncoloured and named in italics.

From 1886 the borders are dated to the day, with colonies and capitals, so every year can be shown, and every month from 1976. The data runs to the end of 2019. Cities appear from 1900 under the names they had at the time.

To redraw history instead, try UCHRONIA.

Keys: ← → the previous or next change | Shift ← → a month or a year | P plays through time | M flat map | + and − zoom | Esc closes

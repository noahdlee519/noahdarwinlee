CHRONIA

An atlas of the world's states and peoples from 3000 BC to today. Move the year at the foot of the page, and click a state or people to read about it.

Until 1400 the borders come from dated maps, so between two of them the atlas shows the earlier one. From 1400 to 1885 they change year by year, with land the yearly data leaves blank filled from those maps, and capitals shown where the record has them. Before the mid-1600s most borders are approximate and are drawn as dotted lines, and peoples and cultures, as distinct from states, are left uncoloured and named in italics.

From 1886 the borders are dated to the day, with colonies and capitals, so every month can be shown. The data runs to the end of 2019. Cities in every period carry the names they had at the time.

To redraw history instead, try UCHRONIA.

Keys: ← → the previous or next change | Shift ← → a month or a year | P plays through time | Shift P plays at double speed | M flat map | + and − zoom | Esc closes

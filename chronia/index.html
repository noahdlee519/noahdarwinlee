CHRONIA

An atlas of the world's states and peoples from 3400 BC to today. Change the date in its frame or on the timeline, and click a state or people to read about it. Borders change by the year before 1648, by the month from 1648, and by the day from 1800.

To redraw history instead, try UCHRONIA.

These files are built, not edited.

Your screenshots live in game/easy maps, game/medium maps and game/hard maps.
Running

    bash game/build-images.sh

resizes and compresses them into this folder, named after each city's "id" in
game/cities.json. Anything you put here by hand will be overwritten or ignored.

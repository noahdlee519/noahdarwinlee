greatercircle

The shortest way between two places, around the countries you choose.

Shortest paths around the chosen countries are exact for the drawn borders: a route of great-circle arcs that bends only at their convex corners, found by A* over the tangent visibility graph. Distances are on a sphere of radius 6,371.0088 km; the direct distance is also given on the WGS84 ellipsoid.

Borders: Natural Earth, 1:50m (public domain). Places: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), OurAirports (public domain), and world-countries (ODbL); the place list here is derived from them under the same terms. What they lack is looked up as you type at Photon by komoot, whose answers are © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

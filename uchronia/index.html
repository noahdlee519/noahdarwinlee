UCHRONIA

Redraw the world as it might have been: move its borders, found countries, rename and place their cities

Every alternate history needs a map. This one starts from the world as it is, cut into its states, provinces and regions: give any of them to any country, draw borders by hand where the regions don't fit, found new countries and name them, and put cities where they might have been. The satellite view shows the land itself, for when the borders are in the way, and the Antique style draws it all like a hand-coloured map of the 1800s.

Your map is kept in this browser as you work. Copy a link to share it, or save it as a file to open again later or on another device.

Keys: V select, B paint, D draw, C cities; M flat map, S satellite, H hide the interface. Shift-drag or Space-drag moves the map while painting, Alt-click picks the country to paint with, Delete removes a picked city, Esc lets go, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Borders: Natural Earth states and provinces at 1:10m (public domain), simplified. Cities: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Satellite view: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation by Reto Stöckli, and close up, NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (public domain). The flat map is a plain grid of latitude and longitude (the equirectangular projection). Antique type: Old Standard TT by Alexey Kryukov (SIL Open Font License).

Finer regions: US counties from the US Census Bureau through Natural Earth (public domain); Chinese prefectures from China's national mapping agency through Revolutionary GIS (PDDL); German districts © GeoBasis-DE / BKG 2021 (dl-de/by-2-0); Canada's economic regions from Statistics Canada (Open Government Licence – Canada); Australia © Australian Bureau of Statistics (CC BY 4.0); Brazil from IBGE; Kazakhstan, Russia, Iran, Saudi Arabia and Algeria © OpenStreetMap contributors, and India and Ethiopia, under the ODbL; most other countries from their national agencies through OCHA and its partners (CC BY 3.0 IGO); all of them gathered by geoBoundaries.

UCHRONIA

Redraw the world as it might have been: move its borders, found countries, rename and place their cities

Redraw the world as it might have been: give regions to other countries, draw borders by hand, found countries and place their cities. Your map stays in this browser; share it as a link or a file.

History asks a historian, Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to write how your map came to be, to redraw the world from a turning point you give it, and to answer your questions, changing the map when you ask or when the history calls for it; undo takes back anything it changes. What you type and a list of how your map differs from ours go to Anthropic to write each reply. Its histories are fiction.

Keys: V select · B give land · D lasso · C settlements · M flat map · S satellite · H hide the interface · Shift-drag moves the map · Esc lets go · Ctrl+Z undoes

Sources: Natural Earth, geoBoundaries, GeoNames, NASA Blue Marble, Terrain Tiles and © OpenStreetMap contributors. All credits and licences

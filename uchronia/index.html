UCHRONIA

Redraw the world as it might have been: move its borders, found countries, rename and place their cities

Every alternate history needs a map. This one starts from the world as it is, cut into its states, provinces and regions: give any of them to any country, found new countries and name them, and put cities where they might have been. The satellite view shows the land itself, for when the borders are in the way.

Your map is kept in this browser as you work. Copy a link to share it, or save it as a file to open again later or on another device.

Borders: Natural Earth states and provinces at 1:10m (public domain), simplified. Cities: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Satellite view: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation by Reto Stöckli (public domain).

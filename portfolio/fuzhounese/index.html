Dictionary · 3,790 words and a Claude assistant

Preserving the Fuzhounese dialect. The world’s first collaborative Fuzhounese–English dictionary. With 3,790 entries and growing, we are also gathering native-speaker audio to train an AI and ensure the sound of the language is never lost.

Technical details

The dictionary has an assistant, powered by Claude, that answers questions in plain language: how to say “have you eaten?”, what a word means, or what tone sandhi is. It sits beside search, so a learner who doesn’t know the spelling can still find the word.

  • Claude
  • Search across 3 scripts
  • Native-speaker audio

Inspiration

Fuzhounese is the dialect my grandparents mainly speak, and there was no English dictionary of it online. I wanted a place to keep its words and the voices that say them, and to make it something anyone could learn from.

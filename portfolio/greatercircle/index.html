GREATERCIRCLE

Globe · Flight paths beyond a straight line

While standard flight maps draw straight lines from point a to point b, this tool calculates the true great-circle distance between any two points, factoring in political geography and user preference. Simply place your points, pick the regions or airspace to avoid, and watch the program plot out the optimal route. It is the perfect companion for aviation geeks, passengers tired of deceptive seat-back flight maps, and aviation personnel hoping to validate their routes.

Technical details

The route improves on great circle distance pathfinding on a sphere by calculating a chain of great-circle arcs bending only at the avoided regions' convex corners, found by an A* algorithm over their tangent visibility graph, in three-dimensional unit vectors.

Borders from Natural Earth; places from GeoNames and OurAirports, with Photon for the long tail. Labeled cities come from a modified GaWC index.

  • Exact shortest paths

Inspiration

I was flying from South Korea to Poland and not long after takeoff, I booted up the seat-back flight map to see that our plane was projected to chart a straight line through four countries with closed off airspaces. As a nervous flier, knowing the accurate flight path would have put me more at ease.

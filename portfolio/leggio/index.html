Chrome extension · Translation with on-device AI

Instant, context-aware translations at your fingertips. Just hold a hotkey and select any foreign word or phrase to see its exact meaning pop up instantly. Leggio analyzes the entire sentence to ensure you get the perfect translation every single time. Supports 34 languages.

Technical details

Leggio translates with the AI models built into Chrome, so the words you select are translated on your own computer, for free. You can plug in your own API key if you’d rather use a different model.

Nothing goes to a server: no account, no analytics, no ads. Saved words stay in your browser.

  • Chrome built-in AI
  • On-device
  • Context-aware
  • Web pages + PDFs

Inspiration

Reading in a language you are learning means stopping at every word you do not know, switching to a dictionary or a translation, and losing the sentence on the way back. I wanted the meaning to arrive beside the word, in the sentence it belongs to, so the reading never stops.

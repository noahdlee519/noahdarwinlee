Leggio

getleggio.com

Read in the language you’re learning, without switching to a translation.

A free Chrome extension. Select a word you don’t know and its meaning appears right beside it. Leggio reads the whole sentence, so a word with several meanings gets the one it has there. It works on web pages and PDFs in 34+ languages.

    The AI inside

    Leggio translates with the AI models built into Chrome, so the words you select are translated on your own computer, for free. You can plug in your own API key if you’d rather use a different model.

    Nothing goes to a server: no account, no analytics, no ads. Saved words stay in your browser.

    • Chrome built-in AI
    • On-device
    • Context-aware
    • Web pages + PDFs

    Fuzhounese

    fuzhounese.org

    A living dictionary for a dialect with few written records.

    A free, collaborative Fuzhounese–English dictionary: 3,790 entries with English and Mandarin, 403 recordings from native speakers in 5 places, and 12 contributors so far. Anyone who speaks it can record a word from their phone.

      The AI inside

      The dictionary has an assistant, powered by Claude, that answers questions in plain language: how to say “have you eaten?”, what a word means, or what tone sandhi is. It sits beside search, so a learner who doesn’t know the spelling can still find the word.

      LoreTour

      loretour.com · private preview

      What happened in this building?

      A map of all 45,000 or so buildings in Manhattan. Pick one and you get its lore: dated entries on what happened there, each citing its source, plus notes from visitors, archive photos and a 3D view. It’s invite-only for now.

      Under the hood

      Footprints, roof heights and years come from NYC Open Data. Satellite and 3D views come from Google’s Photorealistic 3D Tiles, and archive photos open on Urban Archive.

      The record started with researched entries for buildings in every Manhattan neighborhood, drawn from Wikipedia and the city’s building historians. Anyone signed in can add to it.

GREATERcircle

Visit

An interactive globe built for anyone curious about how we really fly. While standard flight maps draw straight lines from point A to point B, this tool calculates the true great-circle distance between any two points, factoring in political geography and user preference. Simply place your points, note the regions or airspace to avoid, and watch the program plot out the optimal route. It is the perfect companion for aviation geeks, passengers tired of deceptive seat-back flight maps, or aviation personnel keen on validating their routes around global conflict zones.

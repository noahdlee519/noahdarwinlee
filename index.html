Noah is a recent graduate of the University of Chicago, where he received his bachelor's in History, Law Letters and Society, and Geography. He is interested in consulates, languages, cities, and legacies. On this website you can find projects ranging from a digital dialect dictionary to paintings of a shopping mall in Missouri to an architectural proposal to honor Czech diaspora.

Masaryk Cultural Center

Reimagining the statue of T. G. Masaryk on Chicago's Midway Plaisance as an active site embodying the Czechoslovak leader’s vision of democracy and the city of Chicago’s Czech diasporic history. Specific project goals were to maintain large-scale site geometry, create symbolic vegetation & terrain, and encourage play & curiosity.
